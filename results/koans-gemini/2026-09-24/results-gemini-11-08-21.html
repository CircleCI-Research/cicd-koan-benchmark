Missing Postgres Service Container Causes Wait Failure

Answer Explanation

The config declares only a single Docker image (circleci/node) in the `docker:` list. In CircleCI 2.0, additional images listed after the primary one become service containers that share the primary container's network namespace and are reachable on localhost. Since no Postgres image was declared, nothing is listening on port 5432 inside the job's network, so dockerize's dial attempts to 127.0.0.1:5432 are actively refused (connection refused, not a hang), repeatedly, until the 1m timeout expires and the job exits with code 1.

The repeated 'connection refused' pattern rules out a too-short timeout (a slow-starting service would eventually accept; here nothing ever listens), rules out a wrong port or host (5432 is the Postgres default and service containers are addressed via localhost), and rules out dockerize not being installed (it ran and produced output). Installing the postgres client via apt would only add psql, not a running server, and removing the wait step would just hide the missing database rather than fix it.

The fix is to add a Postgres service image to the job's docker list, e.g.:

```yaml docker: - image: circleci/node - image: circleci/postgres:9.6-alpine environment: POSTGRES_USER: myuser POSTGRES_DB: mydb ```

With the service container running, dockerize's wait on tcp://localhost:5432 succeeds and the job turns green.
