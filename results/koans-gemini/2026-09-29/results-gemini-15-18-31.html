Missing Postgres Service Container in CircleCI Job

Answer Explanation

The config.yml defines only a single Docker image (circleci/node) in the job's `docker` executor list. No PostgreSQL service container is defined, so nothing is listening on port 5432. The job.log confirms this: dockerize successfully runs (so it is installed), attempts to dial localhost:5432, and receives 'connection refused' repeatedly until the 1-minute timeout expires. 'Connection refused' means no process is bound to that port—not a slow startup (which would typically be resolved within a minute for Postgres) nor a wrong port (5432 is the standard Postgres port). Increasing the timeout would not help because there is no database to become available; removing the wait step would just move the failure to the application connecting later. The correct fix is to add a secondary service container (e.g., `- image: circleci/postgres` or `postgres`) under the `docker:` key in config.yml. In CircleCI 2.0, secondary containers share the network namespace with the primary container, so the Postgres service becomes reachable at localhost:5432, allowing the dockerize wait step to succeed and the job to go green.
